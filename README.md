# Migration Symphony

A generative audiovisual artwork by Karen Frances Eng, a Department of Ontological Cartography (DoOC) work. A year of bird sightings at RSPB Frampton Marsh (2025) becomes movement and sound. GroundWork Gallery residency, the Wash, 2026. Best with headphones.

Open `index.html` through the site's address and tap the screen once to begin. On an iPhone, turn silent mode off.

Each cloud is a species. Each shape is a group of birds. Only migrating birds make sound.

Data: RSPB Frampton Marsh, "Summary of Monthly Bird Sightings 2025" (175 species). The work follows how often each species was reported, not population. Where the birds fly to and from is drawn from known migration routes, not from the record.
